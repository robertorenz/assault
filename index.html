How to play
  • Cross each floating stage and destroy the fortress at the far end. The timer refills at every ridge you cross. Follow the yellow arrow sign.
  • Knock out the five red pyramid guns to expose the core, then destroy it to open the way to the next stage.
  • Power Wheelie rears your tank up to lob a grenade over walls. Rolling sideways dodges incoming fire.
  • Drive into a blue lift ring to rise up and bomb from above. Blue-chevron jump pads launch you over ridges.
