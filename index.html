How to play
  • Fight through each area and destroy the fortress at the far end before the timer runs out. Follow the flashing arrow.
  • Knock out the four fortress guns to expose the core, then destroy it.
  • Power Wheelie rears your tank up to lob a grenade over walls. Rolling sideways dodges incoming fire.
  • Drive onto a lift zone to rise into the air and bomb from above. Jump pads launch you over barriers.
